Instagram Downloader FAQ

Find answers about using AkhiSave to search and download public Instagram photos, reels, videos and stories.

What is AkhiSave?

AkhiSave is an online Instagram downloader that lets you search public Instagram photos, reels, videos, stories and profiles using supported Instagram URLs or usernames.

How do I download an Instagram photo?

Open AkhiSave, choose Photo, paste a public Instagram photo URL or enter a username, then use the available Download option for returned media.

How do I download Instagram Reels?

Choose Reels on AkhiSave and enter a supported public Instagram Reel URL or username. When media is available, select the Download option.

Can I download Instagram videos?

Yes. Choose the Video tool and provide a supported public Instagram video URL or username. AkhiSave displays downloadable media when the connected service provides it.

Can I download Instagram Stories?

Public active Instagram Stories can be searched through the Story tool when they are available. Stories may disappear after their normal Instagram availability period.

Do I need my Instagram password?

No. AkhiSave does not ask for your Instagram password. It is designed for public content and does not support private or login-only content.

Can I download a private Instagram account?

No. AkhiSave supports public Instagram content only. Do not provide passwords or private credentials.

Can I search an Instagram profile by username?

Yes. Choose Profile and enter a public Instagram username or profile URL. Public profile information and available sections can then be viewed inside AkhiSave.

Is every Instagram URL guaranteed to work?

No. Availability can depend on the public status of the content and third-party services used by AkhiSave. Some content may be unavailable, removed or restricted.

Can I download any Instagram content I find?

Only use downloaded media when you own it or have permission to use it. Respect Instagram's terms, copyright and other rights.

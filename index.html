Get your result

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

What is AkhiSave?

AkhiSave is a social media downloader and tools hub. Instagram is the first platform with active tools.

Do I need to log in?

No. AkhiSave is designed around publicly accessible content and does not require an Instagram password.

Which Instagram tools are available?

Photo, Reels, Video, Profile Viewer, DP Downloader, Profile Posts and Profile Reels are available. Story remains unavailable until reliable support is verified.

Will more platforms be added?

Yes, but only when the actual downloader support is reliable enough to offer as a working tool.

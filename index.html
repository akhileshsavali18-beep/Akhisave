How AkhiSave Works

1. Choose Photo, Reels, Video, Story or Profile.

2. Enter a public Instagram URL or username.

3. AkhiSave requests the relevant public data/media from its connected services.

4. Preview the result and use the Download button.

Private or login-only Instagram content is not supported.

Privacy

AkhiSave does not ask for your Instagram password. Search requests are sent to the services required to provide public Instagram data or media. Do not submit private credentials or sensitive information.

Public media may be temporarily processed by the connected APIs. Use AkhiSave only for content you are permitted to access and use.

Terms

Use AkhiSave only for lawful purposes and only download media you own or have permission to use. Respect Instagram's terms, copyrights and other rights.

AkhiSave depends on third-party services and cannot guarantee that every public URL will always be available.

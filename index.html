FAQ

Common questions about AkhiSave.

What is AkhiSave?

AkhiSave is a growing website for practical online tools and guides. The platform currently starts with an Image Resizer and will expand as new tools are ready.

What tool is available right now?

The Image Resizer is currently available. It supports resizing by pixels or percentage, with an option to keep the aspect ratio.

Do I need an account to use the Image Resizer?

No account is required to use the Image Resizer.

Are my images uploaded for resizing?

The resize operation is performed in your browser, so the tool does not need to upload the image to AkhiSave for the resize itself.

Will AkhiSave add more tools?

Yes. The platform is designed to grow gradually, and new tools will be shown as available when they are actually ready to use.

Where will new guides and articles appear?

Published articles will appear on the AkhiSave Blog and can also be highlighted on the homepage.

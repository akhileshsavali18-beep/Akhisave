Instagram DP Downloader FAQ

Can I download a private profile picture?

No. AkhiSave is intended for publicly accessible Instagram profiles. Private profile content is not made available by this tool.

Do I need to log in?

No. AkhiSave does not ask for your Instagram password and is designed around publicly accessible profile information.

Will the DP always be HD?

Not necessarily. The available image quality depends on the profile-picture data returned by the processing service.

What if no profile picture is returned?

Profile-image fields can vary by upstream response. If no usable image is available, the result will indicate that the picture could not be resolved.

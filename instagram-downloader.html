No. AkhiSave is designed for publicly accessible Instagram content and does not ask for your Instagram password.

No. The tools are intended for public content that is accessible without logging in.

Does AkhiSave work on mobile?

Yes. The Instagram tool hub is responsive and designed for touch-friendly browsing on phones, tablets and desktops.

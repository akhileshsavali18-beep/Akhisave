What is an Instagram downloader?

An Instagram downloader is an online tool that processes a public Instagram link and, when supported, returns the available media so a user can save it. Different tools support different types of content. AkhiSave currently focuses on public photos, reels, videos, stories, and profile information.

How to use AkhiSave

  2. Choose the type of content: Photo, Reels, Video, Story, or Profile.
  3. Paste a public Instagram URL or enter a public username where supported.
  4. Select the available result and use the download action.

No Instagram password is requested. Private or login-only content is not intentionally supported.

Why AkhiSave does not ask for your Instagram password

AkhiSave is designed around publicly accessible content and public profile information. You should never enter an Instagram password, authentication code, recovery code, or other private credentials into a downloader website. If a site asks for those details merely to download a public post, treat that as a security warning.

Why a download may not work

  • The Instagram post or profile may be private.
  • The content may have been deleted or become unavailable.
  • The URL may be invalid or incomplete.
  • A third-party media service may temporarily fail.
  • Instagram may have changed access or delivery behavior.
  • The requested content type may not be supported.

When a download fails, check the URL and try again later. Do not provide your Instagram password to solve a downloader error.

Copyright and responsible downloading

Being able to access a public post does not automatically mean you own its copyright. Photos, videos, music, artwork, logos, and other media can belong to their respective creators or rights holders. If you plan to repost, edit, publish, monetize, or redistribute downloaded material, make sure you have the necessary rights or permission.

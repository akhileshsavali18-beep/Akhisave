Instagram Profile Posts FAQ

Can I view private profile posts?

No. AkhiSave is intended for publicly accessible Instagram profiles and does not bypass private-account restrictions.

Do I need to log in?

No. AkhiSave does not ask for your Instagram password.

Will every post be available?

Not necessarily. Results depend on what the upstream processing service returns for the public profile at that time.

Can I save media from the results?

When the upstream response provides usable media, the result page can provide preview and open or save actions.

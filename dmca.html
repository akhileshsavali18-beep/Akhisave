DMCA Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

AkhiSave respects intellectual property rights and expects users of the service to do the same. This page explains the process for reporting copyright concerns relating to material processed through AkhiSave.

1. Copyright Complaints

If you believe that copyrighted material is being made available through AkhiSave in a way that infringes your rights, you may submit a copyright complaint with enough information for the issue to be reviewed.

2. Information to Include

  • Your name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or other identifying information for the relevant material.
  • A statement explaining why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • A good-faith statement that the information in your notice is accurate.
  • A statement that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

3. Review and Action

After receiving a sufficiently detailed complaint, AkhiSave may review the reported material and take appropriate action where reasonably necessary and applicable.

4. False or Misleading Notices

Copyright complaints should be made in good faith. Knowingly submitting false or misleading information may have legal consequences.

5. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that content they download, save, reproduce, or share through AkhiSave is used lawfully and does not violate copyright or other rights.

6. Contact

For copyright-related concerns, please contact the AkhiSave website administrator through the available contact information on the website. Please include the details described above so the request can be reviewed.
